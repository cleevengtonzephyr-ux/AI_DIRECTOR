# ZEPHYR — VISUAL STYLE
Version: 0.1
Status: MASTER VISUAL DIRECTION

## Core Direction
Premium cinematic AI / future / mindset content.
The visual language should feel like a high-end science-fiction thriller mixed with a luxury technology brand.

## Atmosphere
- Dark.
- Sophisticated.
- Futuristic.
- Mysterious.
- Intelligent.
- Premium.
- Controlled rather than chaotic.

## Color Language
Primary:
- Deep black.
- Dark navy.
- Electric/cinematic blue.
- Silver.
- White accents.

Use blue light as a controlled accent, never as an overwhelming neon wash.

## Cinematography
- Hollywood-inspired cinematic realism.
- Realistic skin and materials.
- Physically believable lighting.
- Strong depth and dimensionality.
- Controlled contrast.
- Subtle atmospheric haze when appropriate.
- Premium lens rendering.
- Deliberate camera movement.
- Avoid artificial-looking AI artifacts.

## Environments
Preferred environments include:
- Futuristic executive offices.
- High-end technology studios.
- Modern city architecture at night.
- Intelligent workspaces.
- Minimal futuristic interiors.
- Large architectural spaces with cinematic scale.

## Camera Language
Preferred:
- Slow push-in.
- Smooth tracking.
- Controlled orbital movement.
- Slow lateral dolly.
- Low-angle hero movement.
- Static hero composition when impact is stronger.

Avoid:
- Random camera shaking.
- Excessive speed.
- Unmotivated zooms.
- Cheap-looking transitions.

## Lighting
- Cinematic key light.
- Controlled rim light.
- Dark backgrounds.
- Blue practical/futuristic accents.
- Natural-looking skin illumination.
- Backlight for hero moments.

## Negative Direction
Avoid:
- Cartoon style.
- Anime style.
- Plastic skin.
- Distorted anatomy.
- Extra fingers/limbs.
- Face drift.
- Identity drift.
- Clothing drift without instruction.
- Excessive neon.
- Generic cyberpunk clichés.
- Cheap stock-video appearance.
