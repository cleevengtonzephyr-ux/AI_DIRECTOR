# ZEPHYR — CHARACTER IDENTITY
Version: 0.1
Status: MASTER REFERENCE

## Identity Lock
The ZEPHYR avatar is a single recurring fictional/creative character identity.

### Non-negotiable continuity rules
- Keep the face and identity visually consistent across every scene.
- Do not redesign, replace, beautify, age, de-age, or reinterpret the face.
- Preserve the same head shape, facial proportions, skin tone, eyes, nose, mouth, jawline, ears, and overall physical identity.
- Preserve the same bald hairstyle/head appearance shown in the reference.
- Maintain realistic human anatomy and proportions.
- Do not introduce facial tattoos, scars, exaggerated features, or other identity-changing elements unless explicitly requested in a future project.
- When a reference image is supplied, treat it as the authoritative identity reference.
